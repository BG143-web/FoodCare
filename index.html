About FoodCare

Bridging the Gap Between Surplus and Hunger

Globally, one-third of all food produced goes to waste, while hundreds of millions of people experience food insecurity. Decomposing food waste in landfills is also a primary contributor to greenhouse gas emissions.

FoodCare is built on the philosophy that edible surplus is a valuable community resource. Through simple, transparent technology, we enable rapid coordination so that surplus food arrives on someone’s plate while it is fresh, hot, and dignified.
